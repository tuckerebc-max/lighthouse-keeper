# Design Exemplars

These are patterns to adapt, not authorities over the Navy Yard's Constitution.

| Exemplar | Pattern to borrow | Lighthouse adaptation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Gas Town](https://github.com/gastownhall/gastown) | Named roles, persistent work, handoffs, feeds, patrols, and cross-project coordination | Use structured source windows and role-specific projections instead of asking every role for free-form status. |
| [Gas Town mail protocol](https://docs.gastownhall.ai/design/mail-protocol/) | Handoff messages with context, status, and next; structured health messages | Make structure precede prose: claim, evidence, warrant, uncertainty, and next. |
| [Beads](https://github.com/gastownhall/beads) | Dependency-aware graph work, relations, audit trail, compaction, and session continuity | Read executable work and preserve links; never turn the execution ledger into the institutional archive. |
| [Beads newsletter](https://github.com/gastownhall/beads/blob/main/NEWSLETTER.md) | Explains why a release matters, what changed, and what follows | Treat reports as arguments about institutional movement, not changelogs. |
| [Karpathy's LLM Wiki](https://gist.github.com/karpathy/442a6bf555914893e9891c11519de94f) | Immutable sources, linked synthesis, schema, ingest, query, lint, and write-back | Separate raw source, maintained learning, and outward expression. |
| [claude-obsidian](https://github.com/AgriciDaniel/claude-obsidian) | Source and claim ledgers, contradiction visibility, links, and recoverable transactions | Preserve provenance, contradiction, authority, freshness, and controlled merge into Notion memory. |
| [Dendron](https://wiki.dendron.so/) | Local-first Markdown, hierarchy, schemas, backlinks, Git, and fine-grained publishing | Use portable records and explicit audience/permission scopes. |
| [Open Brain / OB1](https://github.com/NateBJones-Projects/OB1) | Shared, model-agnostic memory infrastructure and typed memories | Make the Lighthouse's memory contract durable across model clients. |
| [LangMem](https://github.com/langchain-ai/langmem) | Immediate capture plus background consolidation and namespaces | Separate operational intake from slower institutional synthesis. |
| [Graphiti](https://github.com/getzep/graphiti) | Temporal context graphs, provenance, validity windows, and supersession | Preserve what was believed when and what later changed. |
