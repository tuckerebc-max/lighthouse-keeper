# Packet-Linked Communication Templates

These templates are for projections attached to an existing `SignalPacket`.
They are not a standalone communications calendar, campaign, newsletter
pipeline, or routing payload. If no warranted packet exists, use the
no-material-signal template or hold the work.

## Eric/Sheryl Navy Signal

```markdown
# Navy Signal | YYYY-MM-DD

## One-sentence signal

What materially changed?

## Why it matters

What capability, decision, risk, or direction does the packet establish?

## Evidence and warrant

- Source: what it shows.
- Reasoning: why that supports the signal in this context.

## What remains uncertain

What the evidence does not establish.

## Implication for Eric and Sheryl

What decision, attention, protection, or review is needed?

## Next move

What should be built, tested, decided, maintained, or reviewed next?

## Linked records

- Signal Packet: [Notion]
- Review or follow-up: [authorized linked record]
- Capability harvest or learning record: [Notion]
```

## Public projection brief

```markdown
# [Title]

## The signal

One clear claim from the approved packet.

## What happened

Brief, concrete account of the work and context.

## What we learned

Evidence-backed interpretation, including limits and counterevidence.

## Why it matters

Implications supported by the packet's scope.

## What we are doing next

Decision, test, build, maintenance action, or invitation.

## What we are not claiming

Explicit boundaries against overgeneralization.

## Sources

Links to the approved Signal Packet and approved public records.
```
